Deals

KFC Deals in the USA

Deals and value meals at KFC change frequently and often differ by market, so we won't list specific offers here that could already be expired by the time you read them.

Why this page doesn't list dollar amounts

Fast-food promotions rotate on short cycles and vary by region and by ordering channel. A deal listed today may be gone next week, or only available in certain states. Rather than risk showing you an outdated or inaccurate offer, we point you to sources that always reflect what's currently running.

Where to find current KFC deals

  • The KFC mobile app, which typically shows offers for your saved or nearby location.
  • KFC's official website, which lists national and regional promotions.
  • Signage at your local restaurant, since some deals are store-specific.
  • Delivery apps you order through, which sometimes run their own platform-exclusive offers.

Types of deals KFC has offered

Historically, KFC in the U.S. has run combo/value bundles, app-exclusive offers, and limited-time promotions tied to specific menu items. The exact names, prices, and availability of these change over time, so treat this as general context rather than a current offer.

Check the app first

Most current KFC promotions are surfaced through the official app before anywhere else, and often require an account to redeem.
