Breakfast Hours

KFC Breakfast Hours in the USA

Here's a clear, honest breakdown of KFC breakfast availability and hours in the United States — without guessing at a nationwide schedule that doesn't exist.

The short version

KFC does not run a standardized breakfast program across its U.S. restaurants the way some other chains do. A limited number of franchise-owned locations choose to serve breakfast items on their own schedule. Most U.S. KFC locations open with their regular lunch and dinner menu and do not offer a separate breakfast menu at all.

Typical opening hours

Many U.S. KFC restaurants open somewhere between roughly 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM, when the regular menu becomes available. Some locations open earlier, particularly those with drive-thru service. Hours are set locally and can change by day of the week, holiday, or region.

Where breakfast is more likely

Locations that do serve breakfast tend to be independently owned franchise restaurants that have chosen to add morning service. There's no published list of which restaurants participate, so the only reliable way to know is to check with a specific location directly.

  1. Use our location search to narrow down restaurants near you.
