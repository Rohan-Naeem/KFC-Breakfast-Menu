Tips for finding a location

  • Search by ZIP code for the most precise results.
  • If you're checking for breakfast, call ahead — not every location offers it.
  • Delivery-app availability may differ from dine-in or drive-thru availability at the same address.

Traveling?

KFC menus and hours can vary noticeably between states and even between neighborhoods in the same city. If you're on a trip, it's worth double-checking the specific restaurant you plan to visit rather than assuming it matches one you're used to at home.
