Pricing

KFC Menu Prices in the USA

There's no single national KFC price list. Here's why prices differ, what affects them, and where to find numbers you can actually trust.

Why we don't publish a fixed price list

KFC restaurants are largely franchise-owned, and pricing is set at the local or regional level. The same item can cost a different amount from one city to the next, or even between two restaurants in the same city. Publishing one nationwide price table would be misleading, so we don't.

What affects the price you pay

  • State and local market — cost of doing business varies widely across the U.S.
  • Individual franchise ownership — owners set their own menu pricing.
  • Ordering channel — in-store, drive-thru, and delivery-app prices can differ for the same item.
  • Combo vs. à la carte — bundling a side and drink changes the per-item math.
  • Ongoing promotions or limited-time deals in that market.

Where to check accurate prices

  • The menu board or kiosk at your local restaurant.
  • The KFC mobile app, which typically shows pricing for your selected location.
  • KFC's official website for that restaurant's listing.
  • The delivery app you're ordering through, since delivery pricing often differs from in-store pricing.
