Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

KFC Breakfast Menu USA ("this site," "we," "us") is an independent, static informational website. This policy explains what data this site does and doesn't handle.

Information we collect

This site is built as a static website and does not run server-side code or a database by default. We do not automatically collect personal information such as names, emails, or payment details unless you voluntarily submit them — for example, through the contact form.

Contact form

The contact form on this site is currently a front-end interface only. In its default configuration, form submissions are not transmitted anywhere. If a form-handling service or backend is connected (see the project README), any information you submit would be used solely to respond to your message.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not include third-party advertising or tracking scripts by default. If the site owner later adds analytics (for example, to understand traffic patterns), this policy should be updated to disclose the specific service used and what data it collects.

Third-party links

This site links to KFC's official website and other external resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party sites. Please review their own privacy policies before providing any information.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page.
